## Ingredients

200g dark chocolate
175g unsalted butter
250g caster sugar
3 large eggs
1 tsp vanilla extract
100g plain flour
30g cocoa powder
pinch of salt

## Method

### Step 1:
Heat the oven to 180C/160C fan/gas 4. Line a 20cm square baking tin with baking parchment.

### Step 2:
Break the chocolate into pieces and melt it with the butter in a heatproof bowl set over a pan of gently simmering water. Stir until smooth, then leave to cool slightly.

### Step 3:
Whisk the sugar, eggs and vanilla in a large bowl until pale and slightly thickened.

### Step 4:
Pour in the melted chocolate mixture and stir until combined. Sift in the flour, cocoa powder and salt, then fold gently until no dry streaks remain.

### Step 5:
Pour the batter into the prepared tin and bake for 22-27 minutes. The top should be set and a skewer inserted into the centre should come out with a few moist crumbs.

### Step 6:
Leave the brownies to cool completely in the tin before cutting into squares.

### Step 7:
Enjoy!
