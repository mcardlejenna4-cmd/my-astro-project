## Ingredients

1 sheet ready-rolled shortcrust pastry
1 tbsp olive oil
1 small onion
150g baby spinach
4 large eggs
200ml double cream
100g feta cheese
1/4 tsp ground nutmeg
salt and black pepper

## Method

### Step 1:
Heat the oven to 200C/180C fan/gas 6. Line a 23cm tart tin with the pastry, trim the edges and prick the base with a fork.

### Step 2:
Line the pastry with baking parchment and baking beans. Bake for 15 minutes, then remove the parchment and beans and bake for another 5 minutes until the base is lightly golden.

### Step 3:
Meanwhile, warm the olive oil in a pan over medium heat. Cook the finely sliced onion for 5 minutes until soft, then add the spinach and stir until wilted. Set aside to cool slightly.

### Step 4:
Whisk the eggs and cream in a bowl. Season with the nutmeg, salt and black pepper.

### Step 5:
Spread the onion and spinach over the pastry case and crumble the feta on top. Pour in the egg mixture.

### Step 6:
Bake for 30-35 minutes, until the filling is just set and lightly golden. Leave to rest for 10 minutes before slicing.

### Step 7:
Enjoy warm or cold!
