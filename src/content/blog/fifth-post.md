## Ingredients

4 ripe tomatoes
1 cucumber
1 small red onion
150g feta cheese
100g Kalamata olives
2 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
1 tbsp red wine vinegar
1/2 tsp dried oregano
salt and black pepper

## Method

### Step 1:
Cut the tomatoes into wedges. Slice the cucumber into thick half-moons and thinly slice the red onion.

### Step 2:
Arrange the tomatoes, cucumber and red onion in a serving bowl. Add the Kalamata olives.

### Step 3:
Whisk the olive oil, red wine vinegar and oregano together. Season lightly with salt and black pepper.

### Step 4:
Pour the dressing over the vegetables and toss gently to coat.

### Step 5:
Crumble the feta over the top and finish with a little extra oregano.

### Step 6:
Serve straight away and enjoy!
